Behind the screen

My approach to game development is pretty simple: find the fun early, and iterate until it feels great. I enjoy bridging the gap between logic (programming) and creativity (design).

Whether I'm tweaking gravity for a hyper-casual platformer, designing diegetic UI for a VR prototype, or sketching concept art, my goal is always to create interactions that feel responsive and rewarding for the player.
